A lesson week, step by step

Whenever you get stuck, there's an app for that moment.

  1. 01

    Find

    welegato

    New in town and looking for a teacher?

    Find teachers, accompanists and ensembles near you on welegato. Write in your own language — the chat translates for both sides.

  2. 02

    Prepare

    LeggiiChiave Soon

    The aria sits a third too high?

    Your scores live in Leggii on your tablet, with every marking from your lessons. Wrong key? One photo is enough — Chiave transposes it into your range.

  3. 03

    Warm up

    for singers
    AppoggioSquillo Soon

    Voice not awake yet?

    Build your breath with Appoggio first, then warm up to piano accompaniment in Squillo.

  4. 04

    Practise

    Pianino

    Need to check your pitch, but no piano around?

    Pianino puts piano, metronome and tuner in one app. Orchestra pitch 443 Hz or Baroque 415 Hz — everything on the same A.

  5. 05

    Record and reflect

    Prova Soon

    What did your teacher say again?

    Record lessons and practice with Prova. The AI sums up what went well and what to work on next.

  6. ↻

    And then: the next lesson

    welegato

    Take everything you built this week back to your lesson. And when you need a new teacher, coach or stage, welegato opens the next door.
