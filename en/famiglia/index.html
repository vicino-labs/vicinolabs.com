A lesson week, step by step

Whenever you get stuck, there's an app for that moment.

  1. 01

    Find

    welegato

    New in town and looking for a teacher?

    Find teachers, accompanists and ensembles near you on welegato. Write in your own language — the chat translates for both sides.

  2. 02

    Prepare

    LeggiiChiave Soon

    The Lied you found is in the wrong key?

    Your scores live in Leggii on your tablet, with every marking from your lessons. If the Schubert song or the arie antiche only exist in another key, one photo is enough: Chiave transposes it into yours — and your teacher and accompanist can transpose to the same key.

  3. 03

    Warm up

    for singers
    AppoggioSquillo Soon

    Voice not awake yet?

    Build your breath with Appoggio first, then warm up to piano accompaniment in Squillo.

  4. 04

    Practise

    Pianino

    Need to check your pitch, but no piano around?

    Pianino puts piano, metronome and tuner in one app. Just set your pitch — 443 Hz like the orchestra or 415 Hz for Baroque music.

  5. 05

    Record and reflect

    Prova Soon

    What did your teacher say again?

    Record lessons and practice with Prova. The AI sums up what went well and what to work on next.

  6. ↻

    And then: the next lesson

    welegato

    Take everything you built this week back to your lesson. And when you need a new teacher, coach or stage, welegato opens the next door. That's how music stays legato.

Who is it for?

The same app does a different job depending on who uses it. Students, teachers, accompanists — everyone needs it.

  • welegatoFind teachers and accompanists in a new city and chat in your own language — it's translated automatically.
  • LeggiiEvery marking from your lesson stays on the score. Review with lesson recordings and AI notes.
  • Chiave SoonOnly found the edition for high voice? One photo and it's in your key.
  • AppoggioA few minutes a day to build your breath. Free, no sign-up.
  • Squillo SoonWarm up every day to piano accompaniment.
  • PianinoFind your notes in a room without a piano, and practise on your own with metronome and tuner.
  • Prova SoonRecord lessons and practice, and the AI sums up what went well and what to work on next.
  • welegatoPut up a profile and students come to you. Get consultation requests and answer right away in the chat.
  • LeggiiA different piece for every student, all on one tablet. Open it straight from IMSLP and mark it up in the lesson.
  • Chiave SoonFound the right key for a student's voice? Transpose the score on the spot and use it in the lesson.
  • AppoggioTurn the breathing work you explain in lessons into homework — students follow the same method at home.
  • Squillo SoonMove the first ten minutes of vocalising out of the lesson. Give the Panofka studies in Pro as homework.
  • PianinoWhatever the studio piano's pitch, play reference notes at 415, 430 or 443 Hz — whatever you need.
  • Prova SoonRecord your own playing and lessons on audio or video, and look back with AI analysis.
  • welegatoSingers and students looking for an accompanist see your profile. Get requests for lessons, auditions and competitions.
  • LeggiiSeveral students a day, dozens of pieces. Plan the order with setlists and turn pages with a pedal.
  • Chiave SoonA student sent the score in another key? Then you need it in that key too — one photo is enough.
  • AppoggioGood accompanying breathes with the singer. Feel where the breaths sit in the music yourself.
  • Squillo SoonNo need to play warm-up scales every time — start on the repertoire with a singer who is already warmed up.
  • PianinoGive the first note even in a green room without a piano, and set the tempo with the metronome beforehand.
  • Prova SoonRecord the rehearsal, then listen again where the tempo wavered and where the breaths fell.

Strong alone, better together

One ID for everythingWith one welegato ID you don't have to sign up again in every app. Leggii comes first, the others follow.
One handwritingEvery app works the same way, speaks the same languages and, wherever there is tuning, tunes within the same A4 = 415–466 Hz.
Free to startEvery app is free to use, much of it without an account. Extras only when you need them.
